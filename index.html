San Francisco Bay Area ❤️ Speed Skating

Bay Area Speed Skating is a non-profit organization dedicated to fostering community and inspiring a passion for speed skating. Through accessible, engaging programs both on and off the ice, we bring inclusive sports initiatives to local schools, workplaces, and neighborhood events.

Bring Us to Your School or Organization!

Empower your students, team, or community with the thrill of speed skating! We bring structured 20-40 minutes programming with interactive athletic demonstrations, fun games, and safety gear directly to your facility.

Interactive PE & Demonstrations

We deliver high-energy speed skating demonstrations and off-ice athletic conditioning modules tailored for PE classes, assemblies, and youth programs.

Complete Gear & Safety Included

We handle everything from equipment setup to essential safety gear and step-by-step coaching, making hosting completely hassle-free for your staff.

Certified US Speedskating Programs

Speed Skating Programs & Clinics

Our programs provide inclusive pathways for skaters of all ages and skill levels—from first-time beginners to competitive junior and masters athletes. Led by US Speedskating Certified Coaches, all gear and safety equipment are fully provided.

Free Event Ages 4 - Adult

Try Speed Skating For Free

Experience the thrill of speed skating! Designed for beginners who know basic forward skating. We supply speed skates, cut-resistant gear, and helmets. Guided by certified coaches.

Duration: 60 Minutes On-Ice

Equipment: Provided at no cost

Prerequisite: Basic ice or inline balance

School Outreach K - 12th Grade

Elementary & PE Modules

Interactive 20-to-40 minute physical education modules brought right to school gymnasiums or local rinks. Includes athletic demonstrations, low-friction balance games, and safety education.

Location: School Gym or Partner Rink

Cost: Free for Bay Area Title I / Public Schools

Staff: Certified Instructors

Weekly Academy Youth & Juniors

Youth Development Clinics

Ongoing technique, cornering efficiency, and speed endurance training for skaters looking to transition from recreational skating into speed skating fundamentals and local meets.

Focus: Blade crossover, edge control, relay starts

Coaching: US Speedskating Level 1/2 Certified

Schedule: Weekly Weekend Sessions

Seasonal Camp All Levels

Short Track Summer Camps

Multi-day immersive ice camps focusing on off-ice dryland conditioning, video stroke analysis, blade bending/sharpening maintenance, and high-tempo track drills.

Includes: On-ice training + Dryland + Blade maintenance

Guest Coaches: Regional & National level skaters

Financial Aid: Needs-based scholarships available

Masters Program Ages 18+

Adult & Masters Speed Skating

Whether you are an adult fitness enthusiast, inline skater crossing over to ice, or a returning speed skater, our Masters sessions provide personalized coaching and fitness conditioning.

Focus: Aerobic fitness, technique refinement, fun

Equipment Rental: Adult long-blade skates available

Leadership Teens & Adults

Coach & Ambassador Pathway

Empowering experienced skaters and volunteers to achieve Skate IA or US Speedskating Coaching certifications. Mentorship and stipend support for youth leaders.

Certifications: US Speedskating Level 1 Prep

Volunteer Hours: High School & College credits

No Equipment? No Problem!

Bay Area Speed Skating Inc. maintains an extensive equipment bank including short track speed skates, safety helmets, cut-resistant neck guards, and gloves. Gear is provided free of charge for all trial events and subsidized for youth program participants.
