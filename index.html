San Francisco Bay Area ❤️ Speed Skating

Bay Area Speed Skating is a non-profit organization dedicated to fostering community and inspiring a passion for speed skating. Through accessible, engaging programs both on and off the ice, we bring inclusive sports initiatives to local schools, workplaces, and neighborhood events.

Bring Us to Your School or Organization!

Empower your students, team, or community with the thrill of speed skating! We bring structured 20-40 minutes programming with interactive athletic demonstrations, fun games, and safety gear directly to your facility.

Interactive PE & Demonstrations

We deliver high-energy speed skating demonstrations and off-ice athletic conditioning modules tailored for PE classes, assemblies, and youth programs.

Complete Gear & Safety Included

We handle everything from equipment setup to essential safety gear and step-by-step coaching, making hosting completely hassle-free for your staff.

Our Vision & Proposed Programs

Programs We Hope to Launch

The initiatives listed below represent the core community programs, youth camps, and free trial events that Bay Area Speed Skating Inc. is working to organize. With your community backing and financial support, we can secure ice time, procure safety gear, and bring these programs to life!

These events are examples of what we aim to organize!

As a newly established 501(c)(3) non-profit, we rely on individual gifts, corporate matching, and donor sponsorships to cover rink rentals, purchasing youth equipment, and insurance. Your tax-deductible donation makes these programs possible.

Proposed Program Ages 4 - Adult

Try Speed Skating For Free

We hope to host introductory community trial sessions providing free speed skates, cut-resistant gear, and beginner coaching to let families experience the sport.

Goal: Free community access for all ages

Funding Need: Rink rental & rental gear stock

Proposed Program K - 12th Grade

Elementary & PE Outreaches

Interactive 20-to-40 minute physical education modules brought right to school gymnasiums. Includes athletic demonstrations, low-friction balance games, and safety education.

Goal: Free outreach for Bay Area public schools

Funding Need: Portable equipment kits & transport

Proposed Program Youth & Juniors

Youth Development Clinics

Ongoing technique, cornering efficiency, and speed endurance training for youth skaters looking to transition into speed skating fundamentals and regional meets.

Goal: Weekly structured junior practice

Funding Need: Dedicated ice slots & coaching stipends

Proposed Program All Levels

Short Track Summer Camps

Multi-day immersive ice camps focusing on off-ice dryland conditioning, video stroke analysis, blade bending/sharpening maintenance, and high-tempo track drills.

Goal: Summer development camps with guest coaches

Funding Need: Scholarship funds for low-income athletes

Proposed Program Ages 18+

Adult & Masters Speed Skating

Tailored training sessions for adult fitness enthusiasts, inline skaters crossing over to ice, or returning speed skaters looking for technique refinement and conditioning.

Goal: Accessible adult health & fitness ice sessions

Funding Need: Adult skate bank & ice reservation

Proposed Program Teens & Adults

Coach & Ambassador Pathway

Empowering experienced skaters and volunteers to achieve Skate IA or US Speedskating Coaching certifications with mentorship and certification stipend support.

Goal: Building a strong certified local coaching base

Funding Need: Certification grants & clinic materials
